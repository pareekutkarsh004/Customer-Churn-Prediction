## 📊 1. Comprehensive EDA Overview & Macro KPIs

The dataset comprises **7,043 customer records** with 21 initial columns capturing demographics, account details, subcribed services, and churn status.

### Portfolio Financial & Churn Summary:
- **Total Customer Base:** **7,043** customers
- **Total Churned Base:** **1,869** customers
- **Overall Portfolio Churn Rate:** **26.54%**
- **Monthly Revenue Lost to Churn:** **$139,130.85 per month** ($1.67 Million annualized revenue loss)
- **Average Monthly Bill Comparison:**
  - Active Customers (`Churn = 'No'`): **$61.27 / month**
  - Churned Customers (`Churn = 'Yes'`): **$74.44 / month** (Churned customers paid **21.5% higher monthly bills**)
- **Average Customer Tenure Comparison:**
  - Active Customers: **37.5 months** (~3.1 years)
  - Churned Customers: **17.9 months** (~1.5 years)

## ⚙️ 2. Features Breakdown & Features Introduced / Engineered

During EDA, the 21 columns in the raw dataset were categorized into specific data types, targets, and predictor roles to prepare for business analysis and ML modeling.

### Target Variable:
- **`Churn`**: Target outcome. Categorical string (`'Yes'`/`'No'`), converted to binary integer (`1` = Churned, `0` = Retained).

### Identifier Attribute (Excluded from modeling):
- **`customerID`**: Unique alphanumeric customer key. Excluded to prevent memorization/overfitting.

### Numerical Features (4 attributes):
1. **`tenure`**: Length of customer relationship in months (Integer range: 0 to 72 months).
2. **`MonthlyCharges`**: Current monthly billing amount in USD (Float range: $18.25 to $118.75).
3. **`TotalCharges`**: Cumulative revenue generated by customer in USD (Float range: $0.00 to $8,684.80).
4. **`SeniorCitizen`**: Binary indicator (0 = Non-senior, 1 = Senior citizen).

### Categorical Features (15 attributes):
- **Demographics:** `gender` (Female/Male), `Partner` (Yes/No), `Dependents` (Yes/No).
- **Core Telecommunication Services:** `PhoneService` (Yes/No), `MultipleLines` (No phone service/No/Yes), `InternetService` (DSL/Fiber optic/No).
- **Add-on Value Services:** `OnlineSecurity`, `OnlineBackup`, `DeviceProtection`, `TechSupport`, `StreamingTV`, `StreamingMovies` (Each with levels: No internet service/No/Yes).
- **Account & Billing Metadata:** `Contract` (Month-to-month/One year/Two year), `PaperlessBilling` (Yes/No), `PaymentMethod` (Electronic check/Mailed check/Bank transfer auto/Credit card auto).

### Features Introduced / Derived During EDA & Preprocessing:
1. **Tenure Cohorts (`tenure_cohort`)**: Grouped numeric tenure into 5 strategic lifecycle buckets:
   - `0-6 Months` (New Onboarding - Highest Risk)
   - `7-12 Months` (Early Adoption)
   - `1-2 Years` (Mid-term)
   - `2-4 Years` (Established)
   - `4+ Years` (Loyal Core)
2. **Service Count Index**: Count of subscribed add-on security and tech support features to analyze protective product bundling effects.
3. **Target Binary Flag (`churn_numeric`)**: Explicit numerical binary flag (`1`/`0`) created in SQL/Pandas to calculate exact rates and means via standard aggregation functions (`AVG()`, `SUM()`).

## ⚠️ 3. Key Challenges & Anomalies Encountered During EDA

During the exploratory phase, several technical data quality issues and analytical paradoxes emerged:

### Challenge 1: Data Type Pollution & Missing Values in `TotalCharges`
- **The Issue:** `TotalCharges` was initially parsed as an `object` (string) column instead of `float64`. Standard `.isnull()` calls returned 0 missing values, yet converting it directly to float failed due to hidden empty whitespace strings (`' '`).
- **Investigation:** Filtering for empty strings revealed exactly **11 rows** where `TotalCharges = ' '`. Further cross-examination showed all 11 records had `tenure == 0` (brand new customers enrolled on the day of data extraction who had not completed their first billing cycle).
- **Resolution:** Rather than dropping these rows or calculating arbitrary mean/median values, `TotalCharges` was coerced using `pd.to_numeric(errors='coerce')` and missing values were imputed as `0.0`, reflecting their true zero-tenure financial state.

### Challenge 2: The "Fiber Optic Paradox"
- **The Issue:** Initial bivariate EDA showed that customers subscribing to high-speed **Fiber Optic Internet** experienced a **41.89% churn rate**, compared to only **18.96% for DSL** and **7.40% for No Internet**.
- **Investigation:** Why would customers with the fastest premium internet churn at double the rate of DSL users? Deep-dive SQL cohort analysis revealed:
  1. Fiber Optic plans had significantly higher average monthly bills ($91.50/mo vs $58.10/mo for DSL).
  2. A large majority of Fiber Optic users were on uncommitted **Month-to-Month contracts** without **Tech Support** or **Online Security** add-ons.
  3. Fiber Optic customers were heavily concentrated in the **Electronic Check** payment method.
- **Resolution:** The higher churn was driven by price sensitivity, unbundled plans, and manual payment friction, rather than service speed issues.

### Challenge 3: Severe Class Imbalance (73.46% vs 26.54%)
- **The Issue:** Non-churners outnumber churners almost 3-to-1. Standard accuracy metrics during EDA could produce a false sense of security (a dummy model predicting `No` for all customers yields ~73.5% accuracy but catches 0 churners).
- **Resolution:** Established baseline evaluation around **ROC-AUC**, **Precision**, **Recall**, and **F1-Score**, while ensuring stratified sampling (`stratify=y`) in all downstream train/test splits.

### Challenge 4: High Multicollinearity in Add-on Services
- **The Issue:** The 6 add-on services (`OnlineSecurity`, `OnlineBackup`, etc.) all shared the category level `"No internet service"`, duplicating the information in `InternetService == 'No'`.
- **Resolution:** Ensured proper categorical encoding (`drop='first'` in One-Hot Encoding) to eliminate dummy variable redundancy before modeling.

## 💻 4. Deep-Dive: SQL Business Intelligence Queries (`sql/churn_analysis.sql`)

The EDA logic was translated into production-grade SQL queries on the SQLite database (`data/churn.db`).

### Query 1: Overall Churn Rate & Macro Financial Baseline
```sql
SELECT 
    COUNT(*) AS total_customers,
    SUM(CASE WHEN churn = 'Yes' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS churned_customers,
    ROUND(CAST(SUM(CASE WHEN churn = 'Yes' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS REAL) / COUNT(*) * 100, 2) AS churn_rate
FROM customers;
```
**Output:**
| total_customers | churned_customers | churn_rate |
| :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 7,043 | 1,869 | **26.54%** |

---

### Query 2: Churn Rate by Contract Commitment
```sql
SELECT 
    contract,
    COUNT(*) AS total_customers,
    SUM(CASE WHEN churn = 'Yes' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS churned_customers,
    ROUND(CAST(SUM(CASE WHEN churn = 'Yes' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS REAL) / COUNT(*) * 100, 2) AS churn_rate
FROM customers
GROUP BY contract
ORDER BY churn_rate DESC;
```
**Output:**
| contract | total_customers | churned_customers | churn_rate |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Month-to-month** | 3,875 | 1,655 | **42.71%** |
| **One year** | 1,473 | 166 | **11.27%** |
| **Two year** | 1,695 | 48 | **2.83%** |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Key Insight:** Month-to-month subscribers account for **1,655 out of 1,869 total churned accounts (88.55% of all churn)**. Switching a customer to a 2-year contract slashes churn risk from 42.71% down to 2.83%.

---

### Query 3: Monthly Revenue Lost & Billing Dynamics
```sql
SELECT 
    SUM(CASE WHEN churn = 'Yes' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS churned_customers,
    ROUND(SUM(CASE WHEN churn = 'Yes' THEN monthly_charges ELSE 0 END), 2) AS total_monthly_revenue_lost,
    ROUND(AVG(CASE WHEN churn = 'Yes' THEN monthly_charges END), 2) AS avg_monthly_bill_churned,
    ROUND(AVG(CASE WHEN churn = 'No' THEN monthly_charges END), 2) AS avg_monthly_bill_active
FROM customers;
```
**Output:**
| churned_customers | total_monthly_revenue_lost | avg_monthly_bill_churned | avg_monthly_bill_active |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 1,869 | **$139,130.85** | **$74.44** | **$61.27** |

---

### Query 4: Top High-Risk Customer Micro-Segments
```sql
SELECT 
    contract,
    internet_service,
    payment_method,
    COUNT(*) AS total_customers,
    SUM(CASE WHEN churn = 'Yes' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS churned_customers,
    ROUND(CAST(SUM(CASE WHEN churn = 'Yes' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS REAL) / COUNT(*) * 100, 2) AS churn_rate
FROM customers
GROUP BY contract, internet_service, payment_method
HAVING total_customers >= 50
ORDER BY churn_rate DESC
LIMIT 5;
```
**Output:**
| contract | internet_service | payment_method | total_customers | churned_customers | churn_rate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Month-to-month** | **Fiber optic** | **Electronic check** | **1,307** | **789** | **60.37%** |
| Month-to-month | Fiber optic | Bank transfer (auto) | 265 | 114 | 43.02% |
| Month-to-month | Fiber optic | Credit card (auto) | 247 | 104 | 42.11% |
| Month-to-month | Fiber optic | Mailed check | 210 | 88 | 41.90% |
| Month-to-month | DSL | Electronic check | 400 | 163 | 40.75% |

> [!WARNING]
> **The #1 Churn Risk Pool:** Customers on **Month-to-Month + Fiber Optic + Electronic Check** suffer a staggering **60.37% churn rate**. This single micro-segment accounts for **42.21% of all churned accounts in the entire company** (789 out of 1,869).

### Query 5: Tenure Cohort Risk Distribution
```sql
SELECT 
    CASE 
        WHEN tenure <= 6 THEN '01. 0-6 Months'
        WHEN tenure <= 12 THEN '02. 7-12 Months'
        WHEN tenure <= 24 THEN '03. 1-2 Years'
        WHEN tenure <= 48 THEN '04. 2-4 Years'
        ELSE '05. 4+ Years'
    END AS tenure_cohort,
    COUNT(*) AS total_customers,
    SUM(CASE WHEN churn = 'Yes' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS churned_customers,
    ROUND(CAST(SUM(CASE WHEN churn = 'Yes' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS REAL) / COUNT(*) * 100, 2) AS churn_rate
FROM customers
GROUP BY tenure_cohort
ORDER BY tenure_cohort;
```
**Output:**
| tenure_cohort | total_customers | churned_customers | churn_rate |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **01. 0-6 Months** | 1,481 | 784 | **52.94%** |
| **02. 7-12 Months** | 698 | 227 | **32.52%** |
| **03. 1-2 Years** | 1,024 | 294 | **28.71%** |
| **04. 2-4 Years** | 1,594 | 324 | **20.33%** |
| **05. 4+ Years** | 2,246 | 240 | **10.69%** |

### Query 6: Service Bundle Impact (Tech Support & Online Security)
```sql
SELECT 
    tech_support,
    online_security,

    SUM(CASE WHEN churn = 'Yes' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS churned_customers,

```

| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |

> **Product Defense Effect:** Subscribing to both **Tech Support** and **Online Security** reduces churn from **48.91% down to 9.01%**—a 5x reduction in customer loss.

## 🎯 5. EDA & Feature Analysis Interview Questions & Answers

### Q1: "How did you conduct your initial Exploratory Data Analysis?"
> **Answer:** *"I broke EDA into three distinct phases: first, data auditing where I identified data types, checked for missing values, and fixed the 11 blank space strings in `TotalCharges`; second, univariate distribution analysis of numerical variables (`tenure`, `MonthlyCharges`) and categorical target balances (26.54% churn); and third, bivariate & SQL cohort analysis cross-tabulating contract types, payment methods, and service add-ons against churn rate."*

### Q2: "What was the most surprising insight discovered during EDA?"
> **Answer:** *"The 'Fiber Optic Paradox'. Fiber Optic users had a 41.89% churn rate compared to 18.96% for DSL. EDA revealed this wasn't due to technical performance, but rather because Fiber Optic was unbundled ($91.50/mo average bill), heavily concentrated on month-to-month contracts, and paid via electronic checks. Bundling Tech Support and Online Security drops churn for these users to under 10%."*

### Q3: "What features did you derive or introduce during EDA?"
> **Answer:** *"I created tenure cohorts (`0-6 months`, `7-12 months`, etc.) to isolate early onboarding drop-off, constructed a binary numerical target flag for SQL aggregation, and engineered add-on feature counts to test the protective effect of multi-product bundling."*

### Q4: "Why did you use `HAVING total_customers >= 50` in your SQL segment query?"
> **Answer:** *"Filtering with `HAVING` ensures statistical significance. Micro-segments with very small sample sizes (e.g., 3 customers where 2 churned = 66.7%) introduce noisy anomalies. Setting a 50-customer threshold ensures that identified high-risk segments represent actionable target pools for business retention teams."*
